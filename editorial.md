# Editorial record

Reviewed 2026-10-10. Full manuscript and all rendered pages read. This is an
agent editorial review, not external peer review.

## Contribution and reader

A methodological argument for separating geometric description, conditional
production feasibility, historical production and the observation record. Its
empirical contribution is a recording-sensitivity study of one archived corpus,
not a global map or a reconstruction method. Archaeologists and computational
researchers can inspect both the inferential distinction and its measured limits.

## Material worth preserving

The opening rim example gives the inverse problem a concrete object without
inventing a fieldwork anecdote. The non-nesting discussion avoids a misleading
four-set hierarchy. The ordinary-PCA control and independent pair resampling
make the empirical argument appreciably more honest. The two-profile construction
is a direct identifiability example; enclosed volume is kept separate from capacity.

## Reverse outline

- A rim and several possible vessels: establishes how completion can acquire the status of observation.
- What belongs in the space: defines the four objects and separates observation from historical explanation.
- What existing maps establish: engages retrieval, selection, D2, disentanglement and observed production without claiming those literatures have solved the same task.
- A recording experiment with Sue ware: states the actual corpus, split, measurements and diagnostic amendments.
- Which relationships survive: reports disruption, sampling noise and the strong compression control together.
- What a completion cannot settle: demonstrates nonuniqueness independently of model performance.
- From a recorded map to a study of making: specifies the independent production and historical evidence a larger programme would need.
- Technical note: makes the computational choices and uncertainty limits inspectable.

## Strongest rival

A compact geometric map may already preserve the relationships needed for a
bounded task. Ordinary PCA's 99.0% complete-map neighbourhood retention supports
that rival here. The paper preserves that outcome and narrows its criticism to
observation loss and unsupported causal interpretation. Descriptor resampling
also explains part of the apparent sensitivity; thinning is not presented as
a pure missing-geometry effect.
