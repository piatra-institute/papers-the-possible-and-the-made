# Brief

## Question

What can a computational morphospace of ceramic forms explain, and which inferences require evidence about making and about the survival and recording of objects?

## Claim and serious alternative

A shape map is conditional on an observation process and a representation. Neither a populated region nor an empty region establishes manufacturing feasibility or cultural preference. A useful research programme must distinguish describable, achievable, historically made and recorded forms. The serious alternative is that robust low-dimensional geometric representations already preserve enough information for the particular historical comparison; the experiment must allow this result.
