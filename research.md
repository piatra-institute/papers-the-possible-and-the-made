# Research and inference record

## Question and evidence tiers

The argument distinguishes representation, feasible production, historical occupation and observation. Its own set definitions and counterexamples are analytical proposals, not historical discoveries. Primary sources establish prior work and particular evidence; the executed study establishes recording sensitivity for one corpus only.

## Primary findings inspected, 2026-10-10

- T1 Osada et al. 2002, pp. 812-813, author-hosted PDF: D2 measures distances between sampled surface points. Their surface-area-weighted sampling differs from sampling an already supplied point cloud. Our quantile implementation is an adaptation, not a reproduction of their robustness benchmark.
- T1 Parisotto et al. 2022, sections 2 and 4.5, arXiv HTML: ROCOPOT consists of published profiles, separates fragment classes and focuses on rims; matching is evaluated with archaeological judgment. It is not a complete-vessel feasibility dataset. One-of-a-kind profiles were excluded in their data preparation; that is especially relevant to interpreting empty regions.
- T1 Tatsuda et al. 2026 supplement, pinned README/CITATION/LICENSE and all 917 input files: five supplied morphological classes, 1024 XYZ+normal samples per file. We consume XYZ only; DCFLIP copies are outside the sample. The accompanying article identity is recorded, but the new paper makes no claim to reproduce its classification performance.
- T1 Nonaka et al. 2024, Results (community and individual variation) and Methods: observations of contemporary production trajectories and final outlines provide a different kind of evidence from an archaeological shape atlas. They do not measure the entire manufacturing frontier. Dryad data were not used: the download failed during planning and the user explicitly chose Sue ware.
- T1 Locatello et al. 2019, PMLR abstract/theoretical result statement: unsupervised disentanglement needs inductive assumptions. Use this to resist naming latent coordinates as independently established causes, not as a theorem that all archaeological inference is impossible.

## Decisions affecting the argument

The study measures sensitivity, not missing-geometry reconstruction. Complete input clouds are references, not pristine original pots. Category labels describe morphology; they cannot establish cultural membership. Actual training splits are source-object groups, not sites or potters. No physical unit calibration is asserted.

The initial whitened PCA model proved sensitive to independent descriptor sampling. The recorded protocol amendment adds ordinary unwhitened PCA and complete-map compression controls while retaining every original result. This diagnostic addition must remain visible in Methods; it is not a preregistered success claim. Confidence intervals describe object-row sampling conditional on a fixed gallery, not independent archaeological populations.

## Strongest counterargument

A representation can be useful without being a causal theory. The manuscript should grant this and distinguish successful retrieval from explanatory identification. Stable comparisons in the raw-scale descriptor are evidence against a blanket claim that computational shape mapping is unreliable.

## Exclusions

No global ceramic intrinsic dimension, automatic functional assignment, inferred historical absence, new manufacturing experiment, or proof of cultural causation. Seed-chat literature not independently inspected is not cited.
